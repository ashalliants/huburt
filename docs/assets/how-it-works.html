How Huburt stays small

A page only exists while you're using it. Everything else is a small background check.

1

You open a service

Its real web page loads (WebKit on Mac, WebView2 on Windows). You use Slack, Gmail or WhatsApp exactly as in a browser.

page loaded: the big cost
→
2

You look away for 5 minutes

The page is closed and its memory is freed. Unread counts and new-message banners keep coming from a light check over plain HTTPS using your own login.

Slack every 30 s · Gmail every 60 s
→
3

WhatsApp has no light check

So it wakes for about 45 seconds every 5 minutes to sync, shows any new messages as banners, and goes back to sleep.

≈ 45 s of every 5 min
Window hidden? Huburt's own interface is closed too. With nothing loaded the whole app is about 26 MB.
Click a banner and Huburt wakes that service and opens the exact conversation or email.
